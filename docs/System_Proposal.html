1Executive Summary

The Applicant Assessment System is a web-based platform that digitizes and streamlines the company’s staff applicant evaluation process. It consolidates all existing paper-based assessment instruments — the Personal Information Sheet, Family Background form, Applicant Questionnaire, Temperament Test, and Emotional Intelligence Test — into a single interactive application.

Interviewers complete the assessment through a guided six-step wizard. Upon completion, the system automatically computes temperament and emotional intelligence scores, summarizes the results, and generates a professionally formatted PDF report that can be downloaded or printed and attached to the applicant’s personnel file.

Key outcome: Reduce manual scoring effort, eliminate misplaced forms, standardize evaluation, and deliver a consistent, professional report for every applicant.

The application runs entirely in the browser with no server or database required. All data remains on the interviewer’s device, and the system works online or offline after the page has loaded once.

2Background & Rationale

Staff recruitment currently relies on several separate paper forms and spreadsheets. Each applicant must be evaluated using:

Existing InstrumentPurpose
Personal Information SheetCaptures basic details, contact information, and emergency contacts.
Family BackgroundDocuments household composition and family member information.
Applicant QuestionnaireGuides the interviewer through 20 structured interview questions.
Temperament TestWord-checklist profiling of four temperament types.
Emotional Intelligence Test45-item Emotional Quotient (EQ-i) style inventory across five composite scales.

This manual process presents several challenges:

  • Time-consuming scoring — temperament totals and EQ scores are tallied by hand and prone to error.
  • No standard report — results are scattered across multiple sheets with no unified output.
  • Document loss — physical forms can be misplaced during a hiring drive.
  • Inconsistent evaluation — different interviewers may apply different scoring interpretations.

The proposed system addresses all of these concerns by digitizing the instruments, automating scoring, and producing a single consolidated report in PDF format.

3Objectives

3.1 General Objective

To develop an interactive, browser-based Applicant Assessment System that captures complete applicant evaluation data and automatically generates a professional PDF report for every assessed applicant.

3.2 Specific Objectives

  • Provide an intuitive, guided interview workflow covering all existing assessment instruments.
  • Automate temperament and emotional intelligence scoring with built-in interpretation bands.
  • Generate a good-looking, downloadable PDF report summarizing the full assessment.
  • Support printing of the generated report for physical filing.
  • Preserve interview progress across page reloads using local browser storage.
  • Require no server, database, or internet connection during use.
4Scope

4.1 In Scope

  • Personal Information Sheet with interview header details.
  • Family Background form with dynamic row management.
  • Applicant Questionnaire (20 interview questions) with comments and suggestions fields.
  • Temperament Test with 4 categories and 80 selectable descriptor words.
  • Emotional Intelligence Test with 45 Likert-scale items across 5 composite scales.
  • Automated scoring, result interpretation, and a review summary.
  • Generation of a multi-page PDF report with download capability.
  • Local data persistence and a reset option for new assessments.

4.2 Out of Scope

  • Multi-user accounts, roles, and server-side storage.
  • Cloud sync, backup, or applicant database management.
  • Integration with external HRIS or applicant tracking systems.
  • Automated applicant ranking or decision-making.
Note: These items are candidates for Phase 2 (see Section 11).
5Functional Modules

The system is organized into six assessment modules plus a report generation engine.

5.1 Applicant Details

Captures personal information (name, date of birth, age, sex, civil status, religion, address, mobile, email) together with emergency contact details and interview header fields (position applied for, date of interview, interviewer). Sex and civil status use selectable pill controls.

5.2 Family Background

A dynamic table records father, mother, siblings, spouse or live-in partner, and children. Interviewers can add or remove rows, and suggested relationships are provided via an autocomplete list.

5.3 Interview Questionnaire

Presents all 20 interview questions with answer areas, followed by interviewer Comments and Suggestions fields.

5.4 Temperament Test

Four color-coded cards (Choleric, Sanguine, Melancholic, Phlegmatic) contain 20 descriptor words each. Clicking a word toggles it, and a live count is shown per card. The highest-scoring type is automatically flagged as the dominant temperament.

5.5 Emotional Intelligence Test

A 45-item inventory (5 scales × 9 items): Interpersonal Skills, Self-Actualization, Social Responsibility, Impulse Control, and Reality Testing. Each item uses a five-point Likert scale. A live score and interpretation badge update as items are answered.

5.6 Review & Download

Displays summary statistics (dominant temperament, EQ rating, interview completion, family members listed) and warnings for incomplete sections. From here the interviewer generates the PDF report or prints it.

5.7 Report Generation Engine

Builds a paginated A4 report containing the cover summary, personal information, family background, full interview transcript, temperament results with interpretation, EQ scores per scale, overall EQ rating, and signature lines. The report is rendered as an image and compiled into a downloadable PDF file.

6Scoring Methodology

6.1 Temperament Test

Each selected descriptor word counts as 1 point for its category. The category with the highest total is the applicant’s dominant temperament.

TableTemperamentDescriptors (20 per type)
1Choleric — The Dominant DriverBold, goal-driven, competitive, takes charge, independent
2Sanguine — The Enthusiastic SocializerEnergetic, friendly, creative, motivator, fun-loving
3Melancholic — The Analytical ThinkerDetailed, precise, orderly, analytical, perfectionist
4Phlegmatic — The Calm PeacemakerCalm, patient, loyal, supportive, good listener
Score / 20Level
15 – 20Strong
10 – 14Moderate
5 – 9Mild
0 – 4Minimal

6.2 Emotional Intelligence Test

Responses are mapped to a 5-point Likert scale where 1 = Strongly Agree and 5 = Strongly Disagree. Each scale is scored by summing its nine items (maximum 45). Lower totals indicate stronger emotional functioning.

Score RangeInterpretationMeaning
0 – 15EXCELLENTEffectively Functioning
16 – 30GOODFunctioning
31 – 45POORNeeds Improvement

An Overall EQ score is derived from the sum of all five scales (maximum 225), applying the same proportional bands (EXCELLENT 0–75, GOOD 76–150, POOR 151–225).

7Technology & Architecture

The system is a pure client-side web application — no server component is required.

Libraries are vendored locally under lib/, so the application does not depend on a live CDN.

7.2 Architecture

  1. index.html — shell containing the sidebar navigation, six step panels, and report canvas.
  2. js/data.js — central question bank: interview questions, temperament words, EQ items, and scoring bands.
  3. js/app.js — application controller: state management, rendering, validation, and wizard logic.
  4. js/report.js — report builder that paginates content into A4 pages and exports the PDF.
  5. css/styles.css — application and print styles.
Compatibility: Works in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari on desktop. A demo mode (?demo) is available for previewing and documentation.
8System Workflow

The evaluation follows a linear, guided process. Interviewers may navigate freely between steps at any time; progress is preserved automatically.

  1. Start — Open the application; a new blank assessment is ready (or a saved draft is restored).
  2. Step 1 — Applicant Details: Encode personal information and interview header fields.
  3. Step 2 — Family Background: Record family members; add rows as needed.
  4. Step 3 — Interview Questionnaire: Answer all 20 questions with the applicant; add comments and suggestions.
  5. Step 4 — Temperament Test: Select descriptor words; the system live-counts each category.
  6. Step 5 — Emotional Intelligence: Record responses for all 45 items; live scores update per scale.
  7. Step 6 — Review: Inspect summary statistics and completion warnings.
  8. Generate: Click Download PDF Report; the system renders each A4 page and saves the file.
  9. File / Print: Optionally print the report or the page using the built-in print view.
  10. Reset: Use New to clear data and start the next applicant.
Validation: The report may be generated with incomplete sections, but warnings are shown and incomplete items are clearly marked as “Not completed” in the output.
9Data Storage & Privacy
  • All entered data is stored locally in the interviewer’s browser using the Web Storage (localStorage) API.
  • No applicant data is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • Assessment progress survives accidental page refreshes and tab closes.
  • The generated PDF report remains entirely in the interviewer’s control and is downloaded directly to the device.
  • Clearing browser data for the site, or pressing the New button, permanently removes stored assessment data.
Caution: Because storage is local, data is not shared between devices or browsers. Transfer reports by downloading the PDF. A shared workstation should be cleared after each assessment.

11Limitations & Future Enhancements

11.1 Known Limitations

  • No multi-device synchronization or centralized storage.
  • No user authentication or audit trail.
  • PDF export relies on browser rendering; heavily styled output is best produced in desktop browsers.

11.2 Proposed Phase 2 Enhancements

  • Server-side applicant database with search and history.
  • Role-based access (HR, interviewer, manager) and electronic signatures.
  • Batch generation of reports and export to spreadsheets.
  • Localized interview questions and response language options.
  • Auto-ranking or candidate shortlist based on composite scores.
  • Integration with scheduling tools and e-mail notifications.
